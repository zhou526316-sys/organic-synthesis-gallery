Title: Harnessing through-space orbital interactions for multicentred organosulfur catalysis

## Editorial thesis

The story is not “another arene halogenation”. The paper proposes spatial organisation and orbital phase among four divalent sulfur atoms as a catalyst-design variable. In the sulfide state the multicentred high-energy HOMO supports enhanced halonium capture; in the halosulfonium state the neighbouring sulfur provides a computed n(S) -> sigma*(S-X) interaction that weakens two-centre S-X holding and supports transfer.

## Evidence ceilings

- OS6: experimental/computational four-sulfur control; important for separating atom count from phase-allowed through-space interaction.
- OS7: in silico control only.
- UV-vis/XANES + DFT: convergent support for the electronic-structure trend, not a single direct imaging of through-space orbitals.
- Kinetics: compatible with/reinforces pre-equilibrium OS1+Br then transfer; not unique microscopic proof.
- OS1+Br HRMS: accessible species under a dedicated low-temperature trapping mixture, not direct standard-catalysis speciation.
- n(S) -> sigma*(S-Br): computational/ETS-NOCV assignment.
- Additive: non-coordinating anion is experimentally important; tight-ion-pair explanation is author model.
- Scope: retain moderate/low yields, altered conditions and halogen-dependent regioselectivity.
