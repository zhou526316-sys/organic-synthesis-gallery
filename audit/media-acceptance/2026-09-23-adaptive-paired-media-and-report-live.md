## Adaptive publication policy is live

Merged PR #172 commit `38e8f0a7c98704fdf65fd91cb1dde218c80941ac` implements adaptive paired-media batching:
- normal target: 20–25 current-corpus ACS articles;
- tail: 1–19 articles may release after the newest eligible staged body record has been quiet for 15 minutes;
- every body article must be paired with a true official TOC, either already public or a strongly DOI-bound current local official capture that is merged earlier in the same Pages build;
- Figure 1 fallback does not count as official TOC;
- max 25 new article identities / 250 new body files per build; existing per-card display limit stays 10;
- no publisher recrawl or stage mutation is performed by the publication consumer;
- prior public automatic images are revalidated/carried forward and corrupt prior snapshots fail closed.

The existing five-minute GitHub preflight remains `.github/workflows/new-body-continuous.yml`. Failure-report outbox delivery is independent of this publication cadence.
