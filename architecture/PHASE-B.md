# Phase B1 — membership fence and browser compatibility (2026-10-04)

Continuation of approved architecture construction. Base: 1734f3c5c0654564884c54a8eb4254efe1659151. This changes only inactive architecture adapters and the existing read-only architecture CI. It does not change public literature, the production reader, userscript, backend routes, media, users, summary content or release times.

The current all-time membership fence validates a byte digest, explicit schema/count, record revisions, monotonic serial and short expiry. Content identity is distinct from the renewable freshness envelope: same-content serials can renew expiry, but changed content, stale serials and replay of an older freshness envelope cannot overwrite current state. Checkpoints retain identity and withdrawals but never retain positive freshness authorization. The transport and trusted clock are injected; a content hash is not an authentication mechanism. No public membership endpoint is invented or deployed here. A newer membership may suppress a withdrawn or corrected record in an old immutable catalog. A newly added DOI missing in that catalog reports catalog-update-required, never a false global absence. After every asynchronous get/search/hot load the reader rechecks the fence. Failure returns verification-unavailable without deleting data. Previously withdrawn DOI cannot be silently restored; authorized reinstatement needs its own future protocol. Revocation is bounded by refresh/TTL, not instantaneous across disconnected clients.

queue-membership.mjs keeps legacy queue.articles intact and validates it against the complete current member set. It filters NEW candidate jobs only, preserving prior scheduler order and every TOC/figure/Evidence flag. Dormant or missing jobs do not erase coverage/checkpoints. A legal in-flight receipt may be retained after retirement; obsolete job IDs are rejected. The adapter returns dispatchEnabled:false; it is not yet injected into the distributed controller.

--save-fixtures on the shadow builder saves only public data to a seven-day validation artifact. Declared LIVE_FILES remain byte-verified against the actual delivery manifest. Public paper-title-resolutions.json is separately double-read and hashed, because it is not part of that receipt's file list. This limitation is reported, not hidden as receipt-bound evidence.
